Privacy policy

Provisional version. A complete privacy policy will accompany the opening of the Djelify service.

This page describes what the djelify.com showcase website, published by LYNORIC SARL, actually does.

What this website does not do

  • It sets no cookies and uses no storage in your browser.
  • It contains no form.
  • It does not measure its audience and uses no tracking or advertising tool.
  • It loads no resource from another website: its fonts are hosted with it.

Hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any host, GitHub may record technical connection data, including visitors’ IP addresses, to keep the service secure and running. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you write to us

If you write to us at contact@djelify.com or call us, we use your address, number and message only to reply to you.

Your rights

Under Ivorian law no. 2013-450 of 19 June 2013 on the protection of personal data, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and object to its processing, by writing to contact@djelify.com. You can also contact the Côte d’Ivoire telecommunications and ICT regulator (ARTCI).
